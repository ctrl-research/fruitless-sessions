A leaky integrate-and-fire model of the MaleCNS v1.0 wiring (Shiu et al. 2024 constants), not a fly. Dots are neurons at their soma positions; a dot glows when the model neuron spiked in that time bin. The body is posed every frame from the same motor readouts that made the notes; nothing is keyframed. MaleCNS is CC BY 4.0, FlyEM at HHMI Janelia and collaborators.
